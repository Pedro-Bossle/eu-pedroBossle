Before you book.

Does whitening damage teeth?

Done with an assessment and follow-up, no. There may be temporary sensitivity, which we manage.

How much is the assessment?

The first assessment has a fixed price and, if you start treatment, it is deducted from the total.

Do you take insurance?

We work privately and issue receipts for reimbursement. Card payments in up to 12 instalments.

I'm afraid of the dentist.

Many people are. We explain each step first and you can ask us to stop at any time.
