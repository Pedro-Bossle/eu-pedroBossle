Frequently asked questions.

Does whitening damage teeth?

Not when done with an assessment and follow-up. There may be brief sensitivity, which we manage.

How much is the assessment?

The first assessment has a fixed fee, and if you start treatment it is deducted from the total.

Do you take insurance?

We are private-pay and issue a receipt for reimbursement. Payment plans up to 12 instalments on card.

I'm afraid of the dentist.

Many people are. We explain each step before doing it, and you can stop whenever you like.
